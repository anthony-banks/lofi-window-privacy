Privacy Policy

Lofi Window for Apple TV — Effective October 22, 2026

The short version: Lofi Window does not collect, store, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. Everything happens on your Apple TV.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect, transmit, or store any personal information, including:

  • No names, email addresses, or contact information
  • No location data
  • No device identifiers or advertising IDs
  • No microphone or camera access
  • No usage analytics or behavioral tracking
  • No cookies or similar technologies

Settings stored on your device

The app remembers a few preferences so it opens the way you left it: the last room you visited, your music station, and whether rain and wind are on. These stay on your Apple TV, are never sent anywhere, and are removed if you delete the app.

No accounts, no ads, no in-app purchases

  • The app has no login or account system.
  • The app shows no advertising and contains no third-party ad or analytics services.
  • The app is a one-time purchase through the App Store; payment is handled entirely by Apple.

Network use

All artwork, music, and sounds are built into the app. It does not connect to the internet and does not send data to us or any third party.

Children's privacy

The app is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, no personal information from children is ever collected, used, or disclosed.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the effective date above.
