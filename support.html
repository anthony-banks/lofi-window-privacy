Lofi Window Support

Cozy illustrated windows with lofi and calm classical music, for Apple TV.

Need help or have a suggestion? Email abanks47@gmail.com and include your Apple TV model and tvOS version if something isn't working.

Using the remote

  • On the house: move between the lit windows and click one to step inside. The basement is through the front door. Press Up from the top windows to reach Settings.
  • In a room: click to show the controls — music station, the song playing (click it to skip), sleep timer, and weather (rain, wind, both, or clear).
  • Play/Pause pauses the music. Back (Menu) closes the controls, or returns to the house.

Common questions

Can it play all night? Yes. The screen stays on while you're in a room. Set the sleep timer (15 minutes up to 8 hours) to have the music fade out and the screen dim on its own.

How do I turn off the rain? Open the room controls and change Weather, or use Settings on the house screen.

Does it need the internet? No. Everything is built into the app.
